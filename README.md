# ClinicianAI

ClinicianAI is an interactive front-end prototype exploring how clinicians can evaluate AI-assisted diagnostic recommendations while remaining actively involved in clinical decision-making.

The prototype presents two predefined clinical scenarios: one with a correct AI recommendation and another with an incorrect recommendation. Users can enter an initial diagnosis, review the simulated AI recommendation and its explanations, accept or reject the recommendation, and provide a final diagnosis and decision rationale.

**Project status:** Front-end prototype developed. AI recommendations, confidence scores and explanations are predefined and simulated. No live AI model or backend is connected.

## Features

- Two predefined clinical scenarios.
- Initial diagnosis entry before displaying the AI recommendation.
- Simulated AI recommendations and confidence scores.
- Contrastive explanations for the recommended and alternative diagnoses.
- Accept and reject decision options.
- Final diagnosis confirmation and decision rationale.
- Ability to edit decisions and review previous completed attempts.


Start the development server:

## Implementation and Scope

The prototype uses React state to manage user interactions and maintain separate progress for each clinical scenario during the current session.

The clinical scenarios, AI recommendations, confidence scores and explanations are stored as predefined data. They are not generated by a live AI model.

The prototype does not use real patient data, provide clinical advice, or perform clinical validation of user-entered diagnoses. User progress is not permanently stored and is cleared when the application is refreshed.

## Limitations

ClinicianAI is an educational prototype rather than a functional clinical decision-support system. Its simulated recommendations and explanations do not represent the output or internal reasoning of an actual AI model. Its limited diagnosis checks do not establish clinical validity.

The prototype has not been evaluated through a clinical user study.
